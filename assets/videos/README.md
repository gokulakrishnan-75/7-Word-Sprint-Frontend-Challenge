# Cinematic Background Videos

Place your looping MP4 video files in this directory:

- `intro.mp4` — Ancient Tamil landscape at night (Fortress, mountains, moon, torches)
- `kingdom.mp4` — Ancient Tamil kingdom at sunrise (Fort, palace, mountains)
- `army.mp4` — King Veeran's massive army preparing for war (Spears, elephants, red banners)
- `palace.mp4` — King Arivan's palace & treasury (Gold, maps, scrolls, scholars)
- `battlefield.mp4` — Epic ancient battlefield (Two armies approaching, dust, smoke)
- `warroom.mp4` — Ancient war room (Wooden table, oil lamps, scrolls, maps)
- `strategy.mp4` — King studying map (Strategic planning)
- `victory.mp4` — Battlefield after conflict at sunrise (Enemy retreating, peaceful dawn)
- `kural.mp4` — Cosmic golden particles & sacred temple atmosphere

### Automatic Fallback System
If any of these video files are missing, the website automatically activates the built-in procedural CSS/SVG shader backdrops, ensuring an ultra-premium visual experience without any broken layouts.
